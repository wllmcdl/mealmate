# \Mealmate

## Your go-to meal analyzer app

This app is made with the ambition of helping people with their nutrition, because we are bombarded everyday with ads
showing
the 'wonders' that some random multivitamin does to your health. But the truth is, in most cases, we don't need pills to
obtain the vitamins we need. In most cases a balanced nutrition is sufficient. But
that's where it gets tricky, we don't know what we are eating. So the technology is here to help us figure things out.
There are many labs working on the constituents of the food we eat, but all that data is not available in a simple
manner, that anybody can understand and use, so
I thought I'd build an app that would be the go-to, to anyone that wants to know what they are eating, and take control
of their health.

### The app will have the following features:

* Make diet plans for resting and workout days
* Show the nutrition information of the food you select in your plan
* Warn about excess or deficiency of some nutrients in your plan
* Make a shopping list based on the food you selected
* Show the price of the items in the shopping list
* Choose how many meals you want in your plan
* Your plan meals can be made for you and delivered to the address of your choosing
* ...
